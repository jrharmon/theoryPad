## A. Still open

### A1. Free-time mode — is "press Enter when done" the right rep boundary? _(decide at the M2 review)_

Agreed to try it and see. Alternatives if it feels wrong in use: a countdown you set ("three
minutes on this"), a repetition count you tick off, or no rep boundary at all — the exercise
just runs until you move on.

### A2. How sparse will the shared pool be, and is 0.75× usable? _(M7)_

Two things M7's review hinges on, neither decidable now.

12 tonics × 7 modes = 84 combinations. If you can realistically fill the 20–30 you actually
practise in, the pool carries the app and generated backing stays deferred indefinitely. The
coverage grid (task 7.4) is what will tell us.

Separately: playback rate makes each track cover a range, which shrinks how many you need — but
only if slowed-down audio is musically tolerable. YouTube preserves pitch, so it should be, but
0.5× and below is where it gets mushy. Worth judging with your ears at M7 rather than guessing.

## D. Out of scope

recording, MIDI I/O, user accounts, multi-user profiles, a mobile phone layout, native apps,
AI-generated exercise content at runtime, importing Guitar Pro files, and standard notation.

docs — which is the point of writing them down.
