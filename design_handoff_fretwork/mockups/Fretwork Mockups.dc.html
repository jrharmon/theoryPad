What it sounds like

Minor, but not sad. The natural 6th removes the darkness of Aeolian and leaves something level and open — cool rather than mournful.

Works for modal jazz, funk, Latin rock, folk in minor keys, and any groove that wants to sit on one chord for a long time.

Writing notes
Signature note · B, the natural 6th. Land on it or hold it and the mode announces itself.
Avoid · B♭. One flat 6th and you are in D minor.
Weak spot · The v is minor, so cadences are soft. If you need a hard resolution you have to borrow A7 from outside.
Compare · Against D Aeolian, only the 6th changes. Play Dm7–G7, then Dm7–Gm7, to hear it.
